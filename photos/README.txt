ROOTS & CRAYONS: PHOTOS FOR THE SITE

Drop your photos in this folder using these exact file names. The page picks
them up on its own; until a photo is here, a little drawing fills its spot.
To see where each photo goes, open the site with ?photos at the end of the
address, for example rootsandcrayons.com/?photos

Size: portrait 4:5 (square for table and birthday), at least 1200 px wide,
JPG under about 400 KB. squoosh.app shrinks photos for free.
Look: daylight, your real kitchen table or fridge, a little mess is fine.
Kids' privacy: hands, and the backs of heads, photograph beautifully.
