      Questions families ask

      Do I need to speak Armenian?

      Not at all. Every card shows the English word and an easy sound-it-out line, and capital letters show which part to stress. You can say each word with confidence the first time.

      Which Armenian is this?

      Eastern Armenian, with pronunciations that follow how many Iranian-Armenian (Parskahayeren) families speak at home. If your family speaks Western Armenian, tell us when you sign up. We're counting requests for a Western edition.

      What size are the cards, and how do I laminate them?

      Four cards fit on each US Letter page, and each card is about 3.6 × 4.9 inches. Print at actual size (100%). On A4 paper, choose “Fit to page.”

      To laminate, cut the cards first, slide them into a pouch with a little space between them, run it through, then trim around each card. The sealed edges last much longer.

      Will they print in black and white?

      Yes. Every pack comes with an ink-saver version made for black-and-white printers. Each group keeps its own shape, so sorting still works without color, and the outlines double as coloring pages.

      Can I use them in my classroom?

      The family packs are for one household. For a classroom, Saturday school or tutoring group, the classroom license covers every pack and both workbooks, with unlimited copies for your students.

      Is anything shipped?

      No. Everything is an instant download, so you can print as many copies as your family needs, any time a card gets loved to pieces.
